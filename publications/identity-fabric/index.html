The Identity Fabric: Governing Delegation in Autonomous AI Systems

Jay Goradia

Read on IEEE Xplore ↗Cite this article ↓

Overview

This article introduces an identity fabric: a distributed identity control plane intended to constrain delegation, preserve intent, and support zero-trust enforcement across interactions between autonomous AI agents.

The central question is how authority should be governed when autonomous systems pass work to other agents.

Explore the topic

Relevant themes include agent identity, delegated access, authorization boundaries, and accountability in autonomous AI systems. Consult the published article for the complete argument and scope.

Related implementation

ScopedAct provides a separate, experimental setting for exploring task-scoped authority and accountable tool access. See its research-to-implementation notes for design influences and boundaries.

The implementation should not be treated as a complete implementation or independent validation of the article.
