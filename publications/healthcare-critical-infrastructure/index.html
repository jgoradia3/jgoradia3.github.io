Building Secure and Trustworthy AI for Healthcare Critical Infrastructure

Pooja Walia · Rajat Rawal · Jay Goradia

Read on IEEE Xplore ↗Read on ResearchGate ↗Cite this paper ↓

Overview

This paper examines the security and trust challenges of AI-enabled healthcare infrastructure, where failures can affect patient safety, data confidentiality, and service continuity.

It presents an architectural perspective bringing together adversarial machine learning defense, federated and privacy-preserving learning, digital twins, and explainable AI.

Research focus

The work considers how security, privacy, and interpretability fit together when AI becomes part of critical healthcare systems. Consult the published paper for the full architecture, supporting discussion, and scope.
