When Systems Act for Users

Jay Goradia

Read on ACM Interactions ↗Cite this article ↓

Overview

This article examines how people can understand and control systems that delegate work to AI agents and other non-human actors. It considers design patterns that expose actions and their authority, give users meaningful permission boundaries, and support intervention when automation goes wrong.

Design focus

The discussion connects interaction design with authorization architecture: interfaces need to communicate the actor, intended task, permitted scope, and available recovery options.

Read the published article for its examples and discussion.
